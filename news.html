Press service news

Announcements of the State Archives: exhibitions, the reading room, the Copy Section and legal deposit. Newest first.

2026

  • 30 October 2026
    New on the website: “On this day”

    The website now has a calendar of dates from the history of Irad, with references to the fonds and to the documents in our online exhibitions. The home page shows the dates of the day or of the coming week.

  • 26 October 2026
    Reader's tickets for 2027 from 1 December

    Reader's tickets are valid until 31 December of the year of issue. Tickets for 2027 are issued at the duty archivist's desk from 1 December 2026, on presentation of the 2026 ticket and a passport or residence card.

  • 8 June 2026
    Seventy years since June 1956: “The June Days” with new documents

  • 5 March 2026
    Opening hours on Irad Day

    On 11 March, Irad Day, the reading room, the Copy Section window and the legal deposit office are closed. File orders for 12 March are taken until 12:00 on 10 March.

  • 19 January 2026
    The Archives in 2025

    In 2025 the reading room issued 486 reader's tickets and received 3,912 visits. The Copy Section registered 1,264 requests and issued 2,031 archival certificates for pensions from the records of the collective and state farms.

    Applicants can now see on the genealogy desk's page how long an enquiry is likely to take, from its registration to our reply, by the region and years of the search.

2020

  • 24 August 2020
    Reading room reopens on 1 September; places by booking only

    The reading room reopens on 1 September 2020. Places are taken by booking only, for the current month; bookings for the following month are taken from the first day of that month. The booking form is on this site.

  • 20 March 2020
    Reading room and exhibition hall closed under the quarantine regime

    In view of the quarantine regime, the reading room and the exhibition hall are closed from 21 March 2020 until further notice. Files ordered for the coming weeks are returned to the stacks.

  • 1 November 2019
    “The Khan's Grant”: Kokand and church charters from 4 November

    On 4 November the exhibition hall opens “The Khan's Grant”, documents from Fond 3, the Kokand charters and church records of Iyık Janyl (1727–1876), the oldest fond in the building.

  • 16 September 2019
    Sound collection digitized
