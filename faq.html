Questions and answers

What readers, applicants and publishers ask most often.

The reading room

Who may use the reading room?

Anyone with a reader's ticket. Bring your passport or residence card and a letter from your institution stating the subject of your research; private researchers write a personal statement at the desk. Foreign researchers also need the written permission of the Director: allow two weeks.

Why are there no free places this month?

The reading room has 20 desks and 4 microfilm readers. Places are booked for the current month, and bookings for the following month are taken from the first day of that month. Book on the first day.

May I photograph documents with my phone?

No. Scanners and cameras are not allowed in the reading room. Copies are made by our staff only: order them from the Copy Section by catalogue reference.

Why was my order slip returned?

Slips are returned when they are handed in after 12:00, when the daily limit of five files is exceeded, when the file is issued on microfilm only, or when the file belongs to records subject to classification review.

Can I see my grandfather's personal file?

Personal files more than 30 years old are open. Files less than 30 years old are issued only to the person concerned or to their heirs, with proof of kinship.

Copies, certificates and genealogy

Why can I not order my request by e-mail?

A request is registered when your stamped addressed envelope reaches the Copy Section with the receipt for the postal order. Without the envelope we cannot send you the reply, so requests by e-mail and by telephone are not accepted.

How do I get a certificate of my work record for my pension?

If you worked on a collective or state farm before 1992, write on form SA-4 to the Copy Section, giving the farm, the brigade if you know it, and the years. Enclose a stamped addressed envelope and the postal order receipt. Pension certificates are issued under the Archives' seal.

Can you send me a birth certificate?

No. The Archives does not issue birth, marriage or death certificates. For a registration made after 1993 apply to the district civil registry office on form CR-2; registers of baptisms and marriages are kept by the parishes and the cathedral archives of Iyık Janyl.

How long will my genealogical enquiry take?

It depends on the region and the years. The genealogy desk's page gives an estimate. Enquiries that name the volost, village or collective farm are answered sooner.

Legal deposit, place names and other questions

How do I get an LD number for a book?

Apply before printing at the legal deposit desk (Monday to Friday, 9:00–12:00, tel. +996-39-2-31-52-40) or by post. Irad has no ISBN agency; the LD number is the book's identifier. Deliver three copies within a month of printing.

My goods were relabelled. Why?

Place names on labels of goods for sale must follow the schedules of the Toponymic Heritage Statute. Labels reading “Talas, Kyrgyz Republic” are replaced. The Toponymic Section's window is open on Tuesdays, 10:00–11:30.

When will the records under review be opened?

When the review is complete, the records will be listed in the Guide to the Fonds and issued in the reading room. Requests for access are entered in the register and answered in the order received.

May I use a document from your website in my book?

Only with the written permission of the Archives. Write to the Director naming the document by catalogue reference and the publication.

Do you hold the archives of the Kurultai or the clans?

No. The sanjyra of the clans of the Autonomous Tribal Regions are kept by the clans themselves, and the roll of the Kurultai by its Chancellery.
