Posts at the State Archives are filled by competition. Candidates are interviewed by the Archives' competition commission.

Open competitions

Archivist, reading room

SA-26/06

Specialist, Toponymic Section

Place of work
Toponymic Section
Grade
Specialist, 2nd category
Applications until
18 December 2026

Checks the place names on labels of goods and on applications for the names of new streets against the schedules; keeps the register of decisions. Higher education in geography, history or philology; a good hand; Iradi, Russian and English.

How to apply

Send or bring to the personnel section, 16 University Street, Talas: an application addressed to the Director naming the post and its reference, a CV, a copy of your diploma, a copy of your work-record book and two photographs 3×4 cm. Questions: +996-39-2-31-52-08, personnel@archives.iradgov.org.

Candidates who meet the requirements are invited to an interview with the competition commission within three weeks of the closing date. The commission may set a short written test (reading a handwritten document of the Soviet period).
