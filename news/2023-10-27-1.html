“Under the Seal”: seals of the Territory and of 2006–2007 from 1 November

On 1 November the exhibition hall opens “Under the Seal”, documents from Fond 110 (territorial legislative journals and enacted statutes, 1992–2006) and Fond 310 (Cabinet of the Democratic Republic of Irad, 2006–2007), arranged by the seals they bear.

The documents of Fond 310 shown are from inventory 1, which is open in the reading room.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
