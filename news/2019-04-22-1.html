22 April 2019

New Guide to the Fonds published

The State Archives has published a new Guide to the Fonds, the first since the move to University Street. It describes the fonds most consulted, period by period, with their dates, the number of inventories and storage units, and the conditions of access.

The printed guide may be consulted in the reading room and in the National Library of Irad. An online edition is published on this site and will be kept up to date.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
