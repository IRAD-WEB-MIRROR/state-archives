Genealogy desk: waiting times on the website

Applicants can now see on the genealogy desk's page how long an enquiry is likely to take, from its registration to our reply, by the region and years of the search.

The desk answers enquiries in the order received and does not receive visitors. Enquiries that name the volost, village or collective farm are answered sooner.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
