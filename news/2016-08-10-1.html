10 August 2016

“The Black Year”: documents of 1916 in the exhibition hall from 15 August

On the Day of the Lost Tribes, 15 August, a hundred years after the flight of 1916, the State Archives opens the exhibition “The Black Year” in the exhibition hall on the ground floor.

The documents come from Fond 21, the records of the Northern Semirechye and Talas districts: the telegram of the Talas district officer of 14 August 1916, the list of households recorded as departed over the passes to China, and a register of families returned in 1917. Transcriptions and translations are given beside each document.

The exhibition hall is open Monday to Friday, 10:00–16:00. Admission is free. The exhibition is also published on this site.
