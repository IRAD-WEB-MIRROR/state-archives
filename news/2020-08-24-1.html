24 August 2020

Reading room reopens on 1 September; places by booking only

The reading room reopens on 1 September 2020. Places are taken by booking only, for the current month; bookings for the following month are taken from the first day of that month. The booking form is on this site.

Reader's tickets issued in 2020 remain valid until 31 December 2020.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
