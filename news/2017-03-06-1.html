6 March 2017

Decrees of the Provisional Government shown for the 25th anniversary of Irad Day

For the twenty-fifth anniversary of the proclamation of the Republic, the Archives opens the exhibition “Decrees of the Provisional Government” on 11 March at 11:00.

It shows documents of Fond 108, the Revolution of 1992 and the Provisional Government, among them Decree No. 1 of 12 March 1992, which repealed the emergency measures, and the ratification of the treaty of 2 June 1992.

On 11 March the reading room is closed; the exhibition hall is open from 11:00 to 15:00.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
