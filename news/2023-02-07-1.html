Toponymic Section: checking place names on labels

The Toponymic Section reminds importers and retailers that place names on the labels of goods offered for sale must follow the schedules of the Toponymic Heritage Statute. Goods marked “Talas, Kyrgyz Republic” are relabelled before sale.

The Section's window is open on Tuesdays, 10:00–11:30. Bring a sample label.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
