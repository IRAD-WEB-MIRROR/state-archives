15 April 2024

Paper conservation course for restorers

A two-week course in the conservation of paper and parchment was held in the Archives' restoration workshop for restorers of the Archives and of the National Library of Irad. The course covered the repair of tears with Japanese tissue, the flattening of folded documents and the boxing of seals.

The files of Fond 3 treated during the course return to the reading room in May.
