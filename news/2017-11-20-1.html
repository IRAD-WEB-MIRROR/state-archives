20 November 2017

Fonds 85 and 86: frequently used files issued on microfilm from 1 December

The microfilming of the most frequently ordered files of Fond 85 (Central Committee of the Communist Party, 1925–1991) and Fond 86 (Talas City Committee, June 1956) has been completed. From 1 December 2017 these files are issued in the reading room on microfilm only; the originals are withdrawn to the stacks for their safekeeping.

Four microfilm readers are available in the reading room; please mark on your booking whether you need one. Prints from microfilm may be ordered from the Copy Section.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
