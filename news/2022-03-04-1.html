4 March 2022

Thirty years of the Republic: the decrees of 1992 shown again

For the thirtieth anniversary of Irad Day the exhibition “Decrees of the Provisional Government” returns to the exhibition hall from 9 to 31 March, with a page of the Provisional President's notebook of May 1992 shown for the first time.

The reading room is closed on 11 March.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
