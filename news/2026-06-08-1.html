Seventy years since June 1956: “The June Days” with new documents

For the seventieth anniversary of the June Days in Talas, the exhibition “The June Days”, first shown in 2006, returns to the exhibition hall from 14 June with documents not shown before: the order of the military commandant of Talas of 17 June 1956 declaring a curfew, and the minutes of the bureau of the Talas city committee of 19 June 1956.

The documents are from Fonds 85 and 86, which are open. Frequently used files of both fonds are issued in the reading room on microfilm.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
