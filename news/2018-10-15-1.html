15 October 2018

Copy Section: requests registered on arrival of the stamped addressed envelope

From 1 November 2018 a request for copies or an archival certificate is registered on the day the applicant's stamped addressed envelope reaches the Copy Section, together with the receipt for the postal order. Requests without an envelope are kept for three months and then returned to the sender, where an address is given.

Requests by e-mail and by telephone are not accepted.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
