Recordings from the sound collection on the album “Voices of the Talas Alatau”

Twenty-four of Aigul Choro kyzy's field recordings from the Archives' sound collection have been licensed for the album “Voices of the Talas Alatau”, released this year.

The recordings were licensed under the Archives' rules for the use of documents: reproduction requires the written permission of the Archives, and the source is given as the State Archives of Irad, sound collection.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
