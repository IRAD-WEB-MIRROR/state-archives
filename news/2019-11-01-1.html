“The Khan's Grant”: Kokand and church charters from 4 November

On 4 November the exhibition hall opens “The Khan's Grant”, documents from Fond 3, the Kokand charters and church records of Iyık Janyl (1727–1876), the oldest fond in the building.

Among them is the inventory of the Church of St. Janyl drawn up on 12 October 1876. The seals are shown from photographs made by the Archives' photographer; the originals are not exposed to light.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
