New on the website: “On this day”

The website now has a calendar of dates from the history of Irad, with references to the fonds and to the documents in our online exhibitions. The home page shows the dates of the day or of the coming week.

A fond is cited only for years inside its dates.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
