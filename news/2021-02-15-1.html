15 February 2021

New edition of form SA-4

A new edition of form SA-4, the request for copies, archival certificates and genealogical searches, is published on e-Irad. The new form has a box to tick for a genealogical search and a line for the catalogue reference (fond, inventory, file, folio).

Requests on the old form are accepted until 30 June 2021. Forms printed on both sides are not accepted.

Press service of the State Archives of Irad
