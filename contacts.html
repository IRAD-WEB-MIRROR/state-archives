Contacts and reception

Requests for copies, certificates and genealogical searches are accepted by post only, on form SA-4 with a stamped addressed envelope. The telephones below are for information.

Written appeals

Written appeals to the Director are sent by post to 16 University Street, Talas, or handed in at the reception desk on the ground floor. They are registered on the day they arrive and answered within 30 days.

Requests for copies, certificates and genealogical searches are not appeals: send them to the Copy Section or the genealogy desk on form SA-4 with a stamped addressed envelope. Appeals and requests sent by e-mail are not registered.

How to get here

About 2.8 km south of Central Square. Buses and marshrutkas to the "University" stop. Visitors to the administration use the entrance from University Street; readers use the entrance from the courtyard behind the National Library (ring the bell once).
