Leadership

The State Archives is a state institution of the Irad Special Economic Zone. It is headed by the Director; the Toponymic Section and the legal deposit office are part of the Archives.

The Director

The Director

Director of the State Archives of Irad

The Director heads the State Archives, signs its orders and the archival certificates issued under the Archives' seal, and gives the written permission that foreign researchers need for a reader's ticket.

Personal reception

First Thursday of each month, 14:00–16:00, room 201, by appointment through the secretariat (+996-39-2-31-52-01).

Deputies and officials

The First Deputy Director

Stacks, conservation, arrangement and description of the fonds

Responsible for the safekeeping of the fonds, transfers of records from state bodies, the inventories and the Guide to the Fonds.

The Deputy Director

Reader services

Responsible for the reading room, the Copy Section, the genealogy desk and the exhibition hall.

The Academic Secretary

Publications, exhibitions and the press service

Edits the Archives' publications and online exhibitions and answers enquiries from the press.
