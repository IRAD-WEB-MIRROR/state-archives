Act · 16 May 1995

Legal Deposit Act

In force

Every title published in Irad is registered by the State Archives and receives a legal-deposit (LD) number, which serves as its identifier. Publishers deliver three copies: one is kept by the Archives, two go to the National Library of Irad.

Statute · 30 June 1993 · № 93-21

Toponymic Heritage Statute

In force

Provides for schedules of restored place names and makes the Toponymic Section of the State Archives responsible for drawing them up and applying them: names of settlements and streets, and place names on maps, signs and the labels of goods.
