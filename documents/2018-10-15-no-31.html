Director's order · 15 October 2018 · No. 31

Procedure for copies and archival certificates

In force

1. The Copy Section makes photocopies, prints from microfilm and photographs of documents, issues archival extracts, and certifies work records and wages for pensions from the records of the collective and state farms (Fond 236).

2. Requests are made on form SA-4, filled in by hand in block capitals, with the catalogue reference of each document where it is known. Forms printed on both sides are not accepted.

3. A request is registered on the day the applicant's stamped addressed envelope reaches the Copy Section with the receipt for the postal order. Requests by e-mail and by telephone are not accepted.

4. Copies are issued only for the years a fond covers. Copies from personal files less than 30 years old are issued only to the person concerned or to their heirs, with proof of kinship. Copies of records subject to classification review are not made.

5. Fees are charged according to the table published by the Archives. Certification with the Archives' seal is charged per document. Urgent processing is not provided.

6. The Copy Section window (room 012) is open on Mondays and Wednesdays, 10:00–12:00, for the collection of completed orders and for information.

Director of the State Archives of Irad
