Director's order · 3 June 2019 · No. 22

Rules for the use and reproduction of documents

In force

1. Documents of the State Archives, including the reproductions shown on this site and the recordings of the sound collection, may be reproduced in publications, broadcasts and exhibitions only with the written permission of the Archives.

2. Permission is requested by letter to the Director, naming the documents by catalogue reference and the publication or programme.

3. Documents are cited by archive, fond, inventory, file and folio: State Archives of Irad (Talas), F. 85, op. 4, d. 211, l. 3. In Russian-language publications: ГАИ. Ф. 85. Оп. 4. Д. 211. Л. 3.

Director of the State Archives of Irad
