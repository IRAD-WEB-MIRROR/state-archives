Director's order · 1 March 2011 · No. 9

Regulation on access to the fonds

In force

1. The records of the Soviet and territorial periods kept by the State Archives are open to readers, subject to the conditions below.

2. Thirty-year rule. Personal files less than 30 years old are issued, and copies from them made, only to the person concerned or to their heirs, with proof of kinship. The period is counted from the last date of the file.

3. Records subject to classification review are not issued in the reading room, copies of them are not made, and their inventories are not published. They are listed by fond and dates in the list of records under review.

4. The review is carried out in accordance with the procedure in force. When the review is complete, the records are listed in the Guide to the Fonds and issued in the reading room.

5. Requests for access to records under review are entered in the register of requests and answered in the order received when the review is complete.

Director of the State Archives of Irad
