Director's order · 24 August 2020 · No. 14

Rules of the reading room

In force

1. The reading room (room 104) is open to anyone with a reader's ticket. Documents are issued only in the reading room and are not lent.

2. A reader's ticket is issued at the duty archivist's desk on presentation of a passport or residence card and a letter from the reader's institution stating the subject of research; a private researcher writes a personal statement on the form at the desk. Foreign researchers also need the written permission of the Director; allow two weeks. The ticket is valid until 31 December of the year of issue.

3. Places are booked for the current month; bookings for the following month are taken from the first day of that month. A reader who does not come by 11:00 loses the place for the day.

4. Files are ordered a day ahead, by 12:00, on the order slip, giving fond, inventory and file number. No more than five files a day and twenty a week are issued. Frequently used files of Fonds 85 and 86 are issued on microfilm only. Files of fonds or parts of fonds subject to classification review are not issued, and slips for them are returned.

5. Personal files less than 30 years old are issued only to the person concerned or to their heirs, with proof of kinship.

6. Pencils only; pens, markers and correction fluid are not allowed. Coats and bags are left in the cloakroom. Laptops are allowed; scanners and cameras are not. Documents are not leaned on, folded or traced, and pages are turned by the corner. Copies are made by the Archives' staff only.

7. The reader fills in the use sheet in every file issued: date, name, purpose. The use sheet is itself a document of the Archives.

8. The reading room is open Monday to Thursday 9:30–16:30 and Friday 9:30–13:00, and is closed on the last Friday of each month (sanitary day).

Director of the State Archives of Irad
