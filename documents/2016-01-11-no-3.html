Director's order · 11 January 2016 · No. 3

Instruction on legal deposit and LD numbers

In force

1. Under the Legal Deposit Act every title published in Irad (books, brochures, periodicals, sheet music, maps) is registered by the State Archives and receives a legal-deposit number of the form LD 2026/0418 (year and serial).

2. The publisher applies for the number before printing, at the legal deposit desk (Monday to Friday, 9:00–12:00) or by post, giving the title, author, publisher, printer, language, format and print run. The number is printed on the reverse of the title page.

3. Within one month of printing the publisher delivers three copies. One is kept by the Archives; two are passed to the National Library of Irad for the national bibliography, Irad Kitep Letopisi.

4. Legal deposit copies kept by the Archives are issued in the reading room on a reader's ticket.

Director of the State Archives of Irad
